# Dashboard Requirements

## Purpose
Build a business intelligence dashboard for SME decision-makers to monitor sales performance and demand trends.

## Key Views
1. Sales KPI overview (revenue, orders, average order value).
2. Monthly sales trend with forecast overlays.
3. Category and product performance breakdown.
4. Regional sales comparison.
5. Customer segmentation (high-value and repeat customers).

## Filters
- Date range
- Region
- Category
- Payment method

## Recommended Tools
- Power BI or Streamlit (local and open-source compatible workflow)
- Source data from `data/processed/retail_sales_cleaned.csv`
