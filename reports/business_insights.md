# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Sales Trends
- Track seasonality in monthly sales and align campaigns to high-demand months.
- Use rolling forecasts to identify likely revenue dips ahead of time.

## Inventory Demand
- Prioritize restocking of top-selling products by region.
- Reduce excess inventory for low-demand SKUs flagged in category/product analysis.

## High-Value Customers
- Identify repeat/high-spend customers and introduce loyalty incentives.
- Use personalized promotions by purchase category and payment behavior.

## Underperforming Products
- Review pricing, placement, and promotions for low-contribution products.
- Consider bundle discounts or discontinuation after sustained underperformance.
